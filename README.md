# Qardis 🫀

**Qardis** is a privacy-focused, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for seamless blood pressure, pulse, and weight tracking. All your data stays strictly on your device.

🔗 **Live App:** [https://k-d-s-z.github.io/Qardis/](https://k-d-s-z.github.io/Qardis/)

---

## ✨ Features

- 📱 **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Installable on mobile and desktop, works completely offline.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First:** All records are stored locally in your browser (`localStorage`). No servers, no tracking, no account required.
- 📊 **Trends & Visualizations:** Interactive charts and numerical summaries (averages, min/max) for customized time ranges (7 days, 30 days, 3 months, or all time).
- 🗂️ **Archive System:** Move entries older than 9 months to a separate local archive to keep your main list and charts fast and clean.
- 📑 **Export & Print:**
  - Export data to **CSV** (compatible with Excel/LibreOffice).
  - Generate and download **PDF reports** directly in the browser.
  - Native print-friendly view for doctors' appointments.
- 💾 **Full Backup & Import:** Easily migrate or backup your data (including app settings and archived records) via JSON files.
- 🌐 **Multi-language & Themes:** Supports English and Polish, featuring dark/light themes and customizable font sizes.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- Plain **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **Vanilla JavaScript** (ES6+).
- Custom Canvas-based PDF renderer and SVG chart generator.
- No heavy external frameworks or third-party dependencies.
